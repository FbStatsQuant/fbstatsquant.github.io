02 · Research

Methods that adapt, scale, and quantify.

Working with Dr. Meng Li at Rice University on nonparametric Bayesian estimation and proximal MCMC.

01
In preparation · Biometrika

Large Bayesian Prior: An Adaptive and Optimal Method

An efficient, fully Bayesian method for nonparametric regression built on large deterministic series priors and global-local shrinkage. It adapts to unknown smoothness and achieves minimax posterior contraction.

series priorsglobal-local shrinkageminimax contraction
02
In progress

Bayesian Joint Quantile Trend Filtering via Proximal MCMC

Joint, non-crossing quantile trend filtering with adaptively learned smoothness, using sequential Gibbs / P-MALA updates and ADMM projections. Simulations show accurate estimates and calibrated uncertainty.

P-MALAADMMnon-crossing quantiles
03
In progress

Local-to-Global Bayesian Computation

A divide–merge–correct framework: run posterior computations locally, then correct global candidates through importance weighting, sequential Monte Carlo, tempering or Metropolis–Hastings. Applied to variable selection and phylogenetics.

SMCtemperingimportance weighting
